Headline

The predictability claim lives in reservations: a tenant holding a reservation pays a fixed fee plus on-demand shape bills for what spills past it, and the question is how much less its monthly bill moves from seed to seed (CV = sd ÷ mean across seeds) than the same tenant's per-token bill over the same runs. The on-demand settings' bills track per-token billing (Predictability, below), so they are the baseline here, not the claim.

Capacity note: burstoff, burst, resv-shared, resv-p95, resv-p95-spill pool runs whose replicas had different KV budgets (Overview, Capacity per replica): read their refusal and start-bound means per profile there before reading them pooled.

floor_violation_rate
floor_violations ÷ ok (the decode floor: first to last token, queue and TTFT excluded)
start_violations

95% CI
two-sided Student-t interval for the mean over the runs of a setting (one per seed); none when n < 2.
paired comparison
setting − baseline on the seeds both have; the t-interval, paired t-test and Wilcoxon signed-rank test are on those differences.
Hedges' g
mean difference ÷ sd of the differences × (1 − 3 ÷ (4n − 5)): the standardised paired effect size.
p (Holm)
Holm–Bonferroni step-down adjustment across the headline rows (headline pairs × headline metrics).
CV
sd ÷ mean of a tenant's bill across seeds under one billing scheme.
billed ÷ quote
a request's shape bill ÷ its shape bound (the quote the tenant could have been shown).
break-even utilisation
where the least-squares line of revenue ÷ GPU cost against utilisation crosses 1.0.
fee share
mean reservation fee ÷ mean slot bill of a reserved tenant across seeds; 1.0 = nothing spilled to on demand.
CV ratio
a reserved tenant's slot-bill CV ÷ its per-token CV over the same runs; below 1 the slot bill is the more predictable.
KV profile
every replica's KV budget in tokens as the gateway's ledger had it at the start of the run (loadrun.json); runs that differ in it ran on engines of different capacity, and a label pooling two profiles mixes them (settings.csv kv_profiles, capacity.csv).
