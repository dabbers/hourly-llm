Headline

The predictability claim lives in reservations: a tenant holding a reservation pays a fixed fee plus on-demand shape bills for what spills past it, and the question is how much less its monthly bill moves from seed to seed (CV = sd ÷ mean across seeds) than the same tenant's per-token bill over the same runs. The on-demand settings' bills track per-token billing (Predictability, below), so they are the baseline here, not the claim.

baseline = k-base (on-demand slot billing with bursting) for settings of the same workload, else the setting's manifest pair of the same workload; every comparison is paired by seed on the common seeds. A price table moves the provider's margin and the reserved tenants' bills; it cannot move anyone's waiting time or floor. An SLA credit refunds a share of the runtime price of a completed request on one of two bases: its decode floor was missed, or it started late (queue + first token over the 2 s start bound, the contract a surge or saturation breaks). The reserved tenant's bill against its per-token bill is reported, not passed or failed. Offline re-pricing holds behaviour fixed: real tenants would change what they buy and send if prices changed, which these meters cannot show.

revenue under per-token billing: reference list prices, lazy at the batch discount
tenant_usd
revenue under shape billing: on-demand shape bills plus reservation fees
reservation_usd
of which reservation fees: slots × duration × the shape's price per second
gpu_cost_usd

output tokens ÷ (replicas used × run hours)
floor_violations
completed requests delivered below 95% of their shape's tps
floor_violation_rate
floor_violations ÷ ok (the decode floor: first to last token, queue and TTFT excluded)
start_violations
completed interactive requests whose first token came more than START_BOUND_S (2 s) after arrival: queue + TTFT
start_violation_rate
start_violations ÷ completed interactive requests (the start bound)

95th percentile of queue + TTFT over completed interactive requests, seconds
refusal_rate
refused ÷ requests
burst_discount_usd

concurrency_p95
95th percentile of requests in flight, one-second bins
preemptions
vLLM preemptions during the run (sampled counter, last − first)
utilisation
seat-seconds sold ÷ seat-seconds offered: Σ over completed requests of billed_s × the seat weight of the shape that took the seat, ÷ (slot-plan seats of the replicas the run used × run duration)
refused_capacity (tenant)

tenant_usd (tenant)

ttft_p95_ms (tenant)

floor_violation_share (tenant)

start_violation_share (tenant)

plan_open_sessions

two-sided Student-t interval for the mean over the runs of a setting (one per seed); none when n < 2.
paired comparison
setting − baseline on the seeds both have; the t-interval, paired t-test and Wilcoxon signed-rank test are on those differences.

mean difference ÷ sd of the differences × (1 − 3 ÷ (4n − 5)): the standardised paired effect size.
p (Holm)
Holm–Bonferroni step-down adjustment across the headline rows (headline pairs × headline metrics).

sd ÷ mean of a tenant's bill across seeds under one billing scheme.
billed ÷ quote
a request's shape bill ÷ its shape bound (the quote the tenant could have been shown).
break-even utilisation
where the least-squares line of revenue ÷ GPU cost against utilisation crosses 1.0.
fee share
mean reservation fee ÷ mean slot bill of a reserved tenant across seeds; 1.0 = nothing spilled to on demand.
CV ratio
a reserved tenant's slot-bill CV ÷ its per-token CV over the same runs; below 1 the slot bill is the more predictable.
KV profile
every replica's KV budget in tokens as the gateway's ledger had it at the start of the run (loadrun.json); runs that differ in it ran on engines of different capacity, and a label pooling two profiles mixes them (settings.csv kv_profiles, capacity.csv).
