Headline

The predictability claim lives in reservations: a tenant holding a reservation pays a fixed fee plus on-demand shape bills for what spills past it, and the question is how much less its monthly bill moves from seed to seed (CV = sd ÷ mean across seeds) than the same tenant's per-token bill over the same runs. The on-demand settings' bills track per-token billing (Predictability, below), so they are the baseline here, not the claim.

baseline = m-affinity (on-demand slot billing with bursting) for settings of the same workload, else the setting's manifest pair of the same workload; every comparison is paired by seed on the common seeds. A price table moves the provider's margin and the reserved tenants' bills; it cannot move anyone's waiting time or floor. An SLA credit refunds a share of the runtime price of a completed request on one of two bases: its decode floor was missed, or it started late (queue + first token over the 2 s start bound, the contract a surge or saturation breaks). The reserved tenant's bill against its per-token bill is reported, not passed or failed. Offline re-pricing holds behaviour fixed: real tenants would change what they buy and send if prices changed, which these meters cannot show.

Definitions

requests
requests the load generator sent in the run
ok
requests completed (status ok)
refused
requests refused: 429 over the shape's context, 503 no capacity
revoked
context bursts aborted because a guaranteed admission needed their capacity
context_bursts
requests over their shape's context admitted as bursts into free KV
output_tokens
output tokens of completed requests
cached_share
prompt tokens vLLM served from its prefix cache ÷ prompt tokens
affinity_hit_rate
stage K: requests placed on the replica that served their conversation's last turn ÷ requests that had one remembered (with the flag off: what placement by load does by itself)
affinity_fallbacks
requests with a remembered replica that ran elsewhere (no free seat, no free KV, unhealthy, ...)
affinity_fallback_seat
of which: the remembered replica had no free seat
affinity_fallback_kv
of which: the remembered replica had no free KV for the shape
affinity_fallback_unhealthy
of which: the remembered replica was unhealthy
replica_request_share_max
the busiest replica's share of completed requests (1 ÷ replicas = even)
replica_prompt_share_max
the busiest replica's share of prompt tokens (1 ÷ replicas = even)
per_token_usd
revenue under per-token billing: reference list prices, lazy at the batch discount
tenant_usd
revenue under shape billing: on-demand shape bills plus reservation fees
reservation_usd
of which reservation fees: slots × duration × the shape's price per second
gpu_cost_usd
GPU cost: gpu_usd_per_hour × replicas used × run duration
shape_rev_over_cost
tenant_usd ÷ gpu_cost_usd; 1 is break-even
token_rev_over_cost
per_token_usd ÷ gpu_cost_usd; 1 is break-even
gross_margin_shape
1 − gpu_cost_usd ÷ tenant_usd
gross_margin_token
1 − gpu_cost_usd ÷ per_token_usd
output_tokens_per_gpu_hour
output tokens ÷ (replicas used × run hours)
floor_violations
completed requests delivered below 95% of their shape's tps
floor_violation_rate
floor_violations ÷ ok (the decode floor: first to last token, queue and TTFT excluded)
start_violations
completed interactive requests whose first token came more than START_BOUND_S (2 s) after arrival: queue + TTFT
start_violation_rate
start_violations ÷ completed interactive requests (the start bound)
start_p95_s
95th percentile of queue + TTFT over completed interactive requests, seconds
refusal_rate
refused ÷ requests
burst_discount_usd
shape bound − shape billed: runtime given away by faster-than-promised delivery
concurrency_p95
95th percentile of requests in flight, one-second bins
preemptions
vLLM preemptions during the run (sampled counter, last − first)
utilisation
seat-seconds sold ÷ seat-seconds offered: Σ over completed requests of billed_s × the seat weight of the shape that took the seat, ÷ (slot-plan seats of the replicas the run used × run duration)
refused_capacity (tenant)
of which 503 capacity refusals
tenant_usd (tenant)
the tenant's bill under shape billing: shape bills of its unreserved completed requests plus its reservation fee
queued_p50_ms (tenant)
median queue wait, mean of the tenant's per-shape × delivery rows
queued_p95_ms (tenant)
95th-percentile queue wait, mean of the tenant's per-shape × delivery rows
ttft_p50_ms (tenant)
median time to first token after admission, mean of the per-shape × delivery rows
ttft_p95_ms (tenant)
95th-percentile time to first token, mean of the per-shape × delivery rows
floor_violation_share (tenant)
floor violations ÷ requests judged (≥ 30 tokens over ≥ 0.5 s): the decode floor
start_violation_share (tenant)
start-bound violations (queue + TTFT > 2 s) ÷ the tenant's completed interactive requests
delivered_tps_mean (tenant)
mean delivered tokens per second over the judged requests
plan_open_sessions
sessions the seed's plan schedules for the open-loop tenants
plan_open_steps
requests (steps) the seed's plan schedules for the open-loop tenants: the offered load
plan_agent_steps
of which the agent tenant's (bursty arrivals: the seed-to-seed swing)
95% CI
two-sided Student-t interval for the mean over the runs of a setting (one per seed); none when n < 2.
paired comparison
setting − baseline on the seeds both have; the t-interval, paired t-test and Wilcoxon signed-rank test are on those differences.
Hedges' g
mean difference ÷ sd of the differences × (1 − 3 ÷ (4n − 5)): the standardised paired effect size.
p (Holm)
Holm–Bonferroni step-down adjustment across the headline rows (headline pairs × headline metrics).
CV
sd ÷ mean of a tenant's bill across seeds under one billing scheme.
billed ÷ quote
a request's shape bill ÷ its shape bound (the quote the tenant could have been shown).
break-even utilisation
where the least-squares line of revenue ÷ GPU cost against utilisation crosses 1.0.
fee share
mean reservation fee ÷ mean slot bill of a reserved tenant across seeds; 1.0 = nothing spilled to on demand.
CV ratio
a reserved tenant's slot-bill CV ÷ its per-token CV over the same runs; below 1 the slot bill is the more predictable.
KV profile
every replica's KV budget in tokens as the gateway's ledger had it at the start of the run (loadrun.json); runs that differ in it ran on engines of different capacity, and a label pooling two profiles mixes them (settings.csv kv_profiles, capacity.csv).
